RECORD 001

2005

FOUNDATION

2005

The Beginning

Detective.co began as a small investigation service focused on helping people resolve problems that were difficult to handle on their own.

In the early years, the priority was simple: build trust through reliable work, careful observation, and results that could be verified.

Every case became an opportunity to establish a reputation based on accuracy rather than promises.

RECORD 002

2010

INTERRUPTION

2010

The Shutdown

After several years of operation, Detective.co was forced to discontinue its activities following a public demonstration that significantly affected the organization.

The service was disbanded and its operations were temporarily suspended.

The period became an important point of reflection on how investigations should be conducted and how trust should be maintained with the public.

HISTORY / 02 OPERATIONS SUSPENDED

RECORD 003

2014

REBUILDING

2014

Earning Trust Again

Detective.co returned with a revised approach to investigation and public communication.

Instead of rebuilding through scale, the organization focused on smaller cases, transparent procedures, and consistent results.

Gradually, confidence was restored through the work itself and through relationships built with the communities we served.

RECORD 004

EXPANSION

2019

A Wider Scope

As the organization grew, Detective.co expanded the types of investigations it was prepared to handle.

Cases increasingly involved digital records, complex timelines, missing information, and investigations requiring cooperation between multiple parties.

Still Investigating

Today, Detective.co continues to handle investigations that require careful observation, structured analysis, and discretion.
